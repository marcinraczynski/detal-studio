Privacy policy

In force from 13 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Detal Studio Marcin Raczyński, ul. Myśliwska 7, 58-570 Jelenia Góra, Poland, tax ID (NIP): 6112484207. For anything concerning your data, write to kontakt@detalstudio.pl or call +48 512 307 933.

What we collect and why

We collect only what you enter in the contact form: your name, email address, optionally your company name, the topic and a description of the project.

We use this data only to reply to your message and prepare an offer. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering an enquiry (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Providing the data is voluntary, but without an email address we cannot reply.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we are discussing the project, and then for no longer than 12 months after the last contact. If we start working together, we keep the data for as long as the contract and the law, including tax law, require.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing purposes. It is accessible only to the providers that help us run the website and email:

  • OVH sp. z o.o. – website and email hosting.
  • Google Ireland Limited – visit statistics (only with your consent).

Statistics and cookies

If you give consent in the notice on the website, we use Google Analytics 4 to see how many people visit the site, where they come from and which parts they view. Without consent the tool does not load and stores no cookies.

Once you consent, Google Analytics stores the cookies _ga and _ga_314TM6Z7DE in your browser (valid for up to 2 years), which make it possible to tell visitors apart. The data collected includes: approximate location derived from the IP address (the address itself is not stored), device and browser type, pages viewed and the time of the visit. We do not combine this data with data from the form.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). The tool is provided by Google Ireland Limited; data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA under the Data Privacy Framework. We keep data in Google Analytics for up to 14 months.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

Apart from that, we store in your browser (localStorage) only the chosen language of the site and your decision about statistics. We do not use advertising tools.

External resources

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts servers and the animation library from cdnjs (Cloudflare) servers. When these files are downloaded, your browser sends your IP address to these providers, which may mean a transfer of data outside the European Economic Area.

The “Book 30 minutes” link leads to Calendly, which has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • have your data corrected,
  • have your data erased or its processing restricted,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing.

Just write to the address given above. You can also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw).

We do not make automated decisions and we do not profile.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
